📖 HOW TO DIVE

• REVERSE POPULARITY: Don't type the obvious answer! Obscure, valid answers sink your Space-Krill 10× deeper in Light-Years (LY).

• ONE ACCEPTED SHOT: Wrong guesses shake the box so you can keep trying—but your first valid answer immediately locks in!

• ONLINE MULTIPLAYER: Create a 6-digit Room Code in ONLINE MULTIPLAYER and send it to a friend on any device to race live! Easier: tap 🔗 COPY INVITE LINK — your friend just opens the link and drops straight into your room, no code typing.

• ⚡ LIGHTSPEED: One 60-second clock that NEVER stops — not even while answers are being judged — and no per-question limit: a prompt stays up until you answer it or the 60 seconds run out. Consecutive accepted answers build your combo multiplier (up to ×5) — a rejection resets it!

• 🔗 CHAIN REACTION: One category forever: each new answer must start with the last letter of your previous answer. Failed attempts and timeouts cost lives — you have 3.

• 💀 LAST SHIP STANDING: Online elimination — same prompt both ships; the MOST COMMON answer each round is eliminated (ties: the slower ship is out).

• ☄️ METEOR STORM: Online survival — the per-prompt clock shrinks every round and you get ONE shot per round. A miss or timeout costs 1 of 3 hull points.

• 🎉 STAR PARTY / 💀 KNOCKOUT / 👥 TEAM CLASH: Party Rooms (inside ONLINE MULTIPLAYER) take 2 to unlimited pilots! Star Party: 10 rounds, everyone answers at once. Knockout: the most common answer and blanks cost hull — last ship wins. Team Clash: 4+ pilots split into CRIMSON and AZURE crews and pool their LY.

• 🔗 CHAIN REACTION (ONLINE): Turn-based 1v1 chain — a fresh prompt every turn, and your answer must start with the last letter of the previous answer. A miss or timeout is a strike; 3 strikes and you lose!

• ☠️ ONE LIFE: Solo sudden death — endless prompts, but a single judged miss or a single timeout ends the run. Chase your personal best!

• 🎨 CUSTOMIZE YOUR KRILL: Open the LOCKER and dress your pilot: pick a COLOR, a HAT, a SKIN pattern, a PET that flies with you, and a TRAIL for your thruster. Your starter look is yours forever — every other color and piece of gear is bought with Star Tokens you earn by flying. Your look shows everywhere — in game, in party lobbies and leaderboards, in duels, and next to your name on friends' screens!
